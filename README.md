# HOLIDAY POP-UP PRODUCT PLANNER

Use this planner to develop a **realistic budget and production timeline** for your Holiday Pop-Up product.

Your goal is not simply to design something great. Your goal is to have a **finished, sellable physical product IN HAND by November 23.**

**Setup:** December 3  
**Holiday Pop-Up Sale:** December 4–6

## HOW TO USE THE PLANNER

**1. Define your product.**  
Describe what you are making, how many you intend to produce, your proposed retail price, and how it will be manufactured.

**2. Research BEFORE you estimate.**  
If you are using an outside manufacturer, look up their actual production times, minimum quantities, shipping times, costs, file requirements, and proof/sample options. If you are making the product yourself, realistically estimate materials, equipment, facility access, production time, and labor.

**3. Build your budget.**  
Include ALL costs—not just the obvious ones. Account for prototypes, samples, materials, manufacturing, shipping, taxes, packaging, labels, and other expenses. The planner will calculate your total project cost, cost per unit, potential revenue, and break-even point.

**4. Build your production timeline.**  
Enter realistic turnaround, shipping, fabrication, and testing times. The planner will work backward from the **November 23 final-product deadline** and help identify when critical decisions and orders must happen.

**5. Pay attention to your Reality Check.**  
The planner will flag schedules that are becoming risky. If your project cannot realistically be completed on time, **change the scope, quantity, vendor, materials, or production method.**

A simpler product that exists is considerably more valuable than an extraordinary product still sitting in a UPS truck.

## SAVE YOUR WORK!

**This planner does NOT save your project online.**

Use **SAVE PLAN** to download your `.productplan` file to your computer or cloud storage.

When you return, open the planner and use **OPEN PLAN** to load that file and continue working.

**Keep your `.productplan` file. It IS your saved project.**

Update your plan whenever costs, quantities, vendors, production times, or other circumstances change.

## REMEMBER

**November 23 means PRODUCT IN HAND.**

Not ordered.  
Not shipped.  
Not “almost finished.”

Your product should be physically complete, inspected, and ready to prepare for sale.

After November 23, your attention shifts to **inventory, pricing, packaging, display, promotion, and installation.**

**December 3:** Setup  
**December 4–6:** SELL THE THING.
